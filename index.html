Who we are

About the Sansthan

Sikhwal Samaj Seva Sanstha, Pune (Reg. No. E-9592/P.2026) is a registered community organisation working to keep Sikhwal families across Pune connected —supporting one another through life's milestones, preserving our traditions, and building a strong network for the generations that follow.

"To unite the Sikhwal community through service, education, cultural preservation, and social welfare while working for the overall development and well-being of society."

Our Purpose

To serve, unite, and uplift the Sikhwal community in Pune through mutual aid, cultural continuity, and social welfare —so that no family in our samaj stands alone.

  • शिक्षा सहायता, भोजन वितरण, उत्सव आयोजन, आध्यात्मिक सभा
  • Social, educational, cultural &economic development of the community
  • Unity, harmony and mutual support among members
  • Assistance to underprivileged families —education, healthcare, welfare
  • Preserving Sikhwal cultural heritage, traditions and values
  • Empowering youth and women through skills &leadership
What we do

Our Activities

Cultural Gatherings

Festivals, Samaj Sammelan and community get-togethers that keep our traditions alive across generations.

Rishta &Matrimony Support

Helping families connect for marriage alliances within the samaj through our directory and community network.

Education &Scholarships

Encouraging our students with guidance, mentorship, and support for higher studies.

Welfare &Mutual Aid

Standing by member families in times of need —medical, financial, or social support.

Samaj Directory

A growing directory of Sikhwal families in Pune —for connection, networking and community strength.

New Member Onboarding

New to Pune? We help Sikhwal families settle in and connect with the community here.
